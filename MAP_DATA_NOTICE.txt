Map data: Natural Earth, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

This prototype uses a low-resolution Natural Earth geometry snapshot distributed
with the installed pyogrio test fixtures (naturalearth_lowres). Only map geometry
and minimal naming fields are included. Population and GDP fields are omitted.
Geometry is rounded to 5 decimal places for packaging; this does not increase
source accuracy. The snapshot is not a statement of current or historical borders.

The country/region matching and camera logic are a local UI experiment, not a
complete geocoding service or a verified historical geography dataset.
